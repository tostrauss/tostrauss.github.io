Theory and deployment, same person

AI researcher and full-stack engineer based in Linz, Austria.

I'm an AI researcher and full-stack engineer. After graduating summa cum laude in Computer Information Systems with a minor in Data Analytics from Post University (CT, USA) in May 2025, I returned to Austria for my MSc in Artificial Intelligence at JKU Linz, where I also research at the Institute for Machine Learning.

My work sits at the intersection of research and production. At JKU I develop and compare explainable AI methods — saliency maps, LIME, SHAP — to make neural network decisions transparent and debuggable. Outside the lab I ship products: I co-founded CollegeRecruit (live on the App Store and Google Play), built and launched JAMIE — a social activity app now on both stores with 500+ users — and run Gastropoly, an operations platform for independent gastronomy businesses that has been in production since July 2026.

I think the best AI engineers understand both halves of the job. Whether it's implementing a GPT-style transformer from scratch, designing a PostgreSQL schema that survives real traffic, or getting an app through App Store review — I bring the same rigour to all of it.

When I'm not training models or shipping features, you'll find me on the tennis court, travelling, or cooking something new.
